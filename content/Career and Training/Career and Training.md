# Career and Training Path
We will now discuss the longevity of a seemingly brand new field. The general career and training paths it takes to get there and what our specialty looks like outside of the military. Electronic Warfare is considered to be one of the fastest growing disciplines within the defense and military space. While still fairly "new" it has received a serious revamp as of late. As the new age warfare continues to trend to the usage of the electromagnetic spectrum, the demand for the aptitude and effective force within the EW realm needs to be filled on both the military and contracting and government side. Traditionally the path to a long career in EW may begin with military service pipelines to commercial, government agencies, aerospace, and defense contractors do exists and job opportunities are growing rapidly.
