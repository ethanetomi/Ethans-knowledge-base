# Electromagnetic Support Index

Welcome to the Electromagnetic Support Directory.

Electromagnetic Support provides the information that allows Electronic Warfare operations to succeed. By detecting, identifying, and locating electromagnetic emissions, military forces gain situational awareness before deciding how to respond. These pages explain the technologies and processes used to support battlefield decision making.
