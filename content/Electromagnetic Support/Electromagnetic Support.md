# Electromagnetic Support

Electromagnetic Support is the portion of Electronic Warfare that focuses on searching for, detecting, identifying, and locating sources of electromagnetic energy. The main purpose is to give commanders a better understanding of what is occurring within the electromagnetic environment. Unlike [[Electronic Attack]], which directly interferes with enemy systems, Electromagnetic Support gathers the information needed to determine what signals are present and what actions should be taken.

## Support to Military Operations

Electromagnetic Support can identify enemy radios, radar systems, data links, and other electronic emitters. Once a signal is detected, trained personnel analyze its characteristics to determine what type of system may be transmitting and whether it presents a threat. This information can support:

- Situational awareness
- Threat warning
- Intelligence collection
- Target development
- Mission planning
- Force protection

A strong support capability allows friendly forces to make decisions based on what the adversary is transmitting rather than relying only on visual observation.

## Relationship to Electronic Warfare

Electromagnetic Support connects directly to the other major portions of Electronic Warfare. Information collected through support operations may be used by [[Electronic Attack]] personnel to select a target for jamming or deception. The same information may also help [[Electromagnetic Defense]] personnel adjust friendly systems to avoid detection or interference. Because of this, Electromagnetic Support often serves as the starting point for a larger Electronic Warfare operation.

## Importance

Modern battlefields contain thousands of electronic signals that may belong to friendly, civilian, or enemy systems. The ability to separate these signals and determine which ones matter can give military forces an advantage before direct contact ever occurs. Electromagnetic Support helps reduce uncertainty and allows commanders to plan operations with a clearer understanding of the electromagnetic environment.
