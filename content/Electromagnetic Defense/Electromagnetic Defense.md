# Electromagnetic Defense

Electromagnetic Defense (ED), sometimes referred to as Electronic Protection (EP), consists of the actions taken to ensure friendly forces can continue using the electromagnetic spectrum despite enemy interference. Rather than disrupting an adversary like [[Electronic Attack]], Electromagnetic Defense focuses on protecting communications, radar systems, navigation equipment, and other mission-critical electronics from jamming, interception, deception, and electromagnetic attack. Together with [[Electromagnetic Support]], it forms one of the three core functions of modern Electronic Warfare.

## Primary Objectives

Electronic Defense is designed to keep friendly systems operational throughout military operations. Its primary goals include:

- Maintaining reliable communications
- Protecting radar and sensor systems
- Reducing vulnerability to jamming
- Preventing electronic deception
- Improving battlefield survivability
- Ensuring mission continuity

Modern military operations depend heavily on electronic systems. If these systems fail because of enemy interference, commanders may lose communications, navigation, intelligence, or situational awareness. For this reason, Electronic Defense has become just as important as Electronic Attack during modern conflicts (Annulli, n.d.).

## Major Defensive Techniques

Several defensive techniques work together to protect friendly forces from electromagnetic threats.

### Electronic Protection

Electronic Protection focuses on operational procedures and technologies that reduce an adversary's ability to detect, jam, or interfere with friendly systems. Techniques such as frequency hopping, emission control (EMCON), encryption, and directional antennas help maintain communications while reducing electronic signatures.

### Hardening Electronic Systems

Hardening strengthens military equipment against electromagnetic interference and directed energy attacks. Shielding, signal filtering, redundant communications, and hardened electronics improve system survivability even when operating inside heavily contested electromagnetic environments.

Although primarily an [[Electromagnetic Support]] capability, Direction Finding contributes directly to Electronic Defense by locating hostile transmitters before they can threaten friendly systems. ## Relationship to Electronic Warfare

Electronic Defense cannot operate independently. It works alongside the other two Electronic Warfare functions to create a complete battlefield capability.

- [[Electromagnetic Support]] detects and identifies electromagnetic threats.
- [[Electronic Protection]] protects friendly systems against those threats.
- [[Electronic Attack]] disrupts or destroys enemy electronic capabilities.

Together these three functions allow commanders to understand, protect, and dominate the electromagnetic spectrum during military operations.
