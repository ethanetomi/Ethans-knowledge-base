## Electronic Protection Techniques

Electronic Protection (EP) consists of the procedures, technologies, and operational practices used to protect friendly equipment from the effects of Electronic Attack. Instead of focusing solely on stronger hardware, Electronic Protection also includes how operators use equipment in combat. Proper planning, signal management, and electromagnetic discipline all contribute to mission success (EMSOPEDIA, n.d.).

## Defensive Techniques

Military units employ numerous defensive measures before and during combat operations, including:

- Changing frequencies through frequency hopping
- Reducing unnecessary radio transmissions
- Using directional antennas
- Emission control (EMCON)
- Encrypting communications
- Monitoring the electromagnetic spectrum for threats

These methods reduce an enemy's ability to detect, jam, or locate friendly forces while maintaining reliable communications.

## Relationship to Electronic Warfare

Electronic Protection works alongside [[Electronic Attack]] and [[Electromagnetic Support]] as one of the three primary divisions of Electronic Warfare. Information gathered through Electromagnetic Support allows operators to identify hostile emitters, while Electronic Protection uses that information to reduce vulnerabilities before attacks occur. Together these capabilities improve battlefield survivability and increase mission effectiveness (EMSOPEDIA, n.d.).
