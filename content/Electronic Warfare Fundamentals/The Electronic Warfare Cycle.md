# The Electronic Warfare Cycle

Electronic Warfare is rarely a single action. Instead, it follows a continuous cycle of detecting, analyzing, deciding, and acting upon information gathered throughout the electromagnetic spectrum. This cycle allows military forces to respond quickly to changing battlefield conditions.

## Typical Process

1. Detect signals through [[Electromagnetic Support]]
2. Analyze the source and identify potential threats.
3. Decide whether to monitor, avoid, protect against, or attack the emitter.
4. Execute Electronic Attack or Electronic Defense measures.
5. Continue monitoring to evaluate effectiveness.

Because the electromagnetic environment changes constantly, this process repeats throughout an operation.

## Decision Making

Commanders rely heavily on Electronic Warfare specialists to provide accurate information before making tactical decisions. Proper identification of signals reduces unnecessary risks while improving mission success. Whether supporting aircraft, ground forces, or naval operations, the Electronic Warfare cycle ensures commanders understand what is happening across the electromagnetic spectrum before committing additional resources.
