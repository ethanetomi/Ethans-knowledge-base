# Electromagnetic Spectrum

The electromagnetic spectrum is the collection of all electromagnetic waves, ranging from extremely low-frequency radio waves to high-frequency gamma rays. Although these frequencies differ in wavelength and energy, they all travel at the speed of light and are used for different purposes. Modern society depends heavily on the spectrum for communication, transportation, entertainment, medicine, and scientific research.

## Why It Matters

The electromagnetic spectrum is one of the most valuable shared resources in both civilian life and military operations. Civilian users rely on it for wireless communication and navigation, while military organizations use it for radar, intelligence collection, satellite communications, targeting, and Electronic Warfare. Because the spectrum is limited, governments carefully regulate its use while military forces actively compete for access during conflict.

## Civilian vs Military

Although both civilian and military organizations utilize the same spectrum, their objectives differ significantly. Civilian users prioritize reliability, speed, and efficiency for everyday communications. Military organizations focus on protecting friendly communications while disrupting those of the adversary. Understanding these differences provides the foundation for topics discussed throughout this knowledge base.
