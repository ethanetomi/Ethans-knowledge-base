# Electronic Warfare Knowledge Base

Welcome to the Electronic Warfare Knowledge Base.

This website serves as an educational resource covering the fundamentals of Electronic Warfare (EW), the electromagnetic spectrum, military applications, defensive technologies, and civilian career opportunities. The goal is to provide readers with a structured introduction to how modern military forces use the electromagnetic spectrum to detect, protect, exploit, and attack electronic systems.

Whether you are new to Electronic Warfare or looking to understand how its major components work together, the following sections provide an organized starting point.
